## 8. Root Cause Analysis: The Tier 1 Spike Anomaly

### 8.1. The Mathematical Flaw in Current Tier 1
In `model/detect.py` line 309 and `run_step16_validation.py`:
$$\text{jump\_mag} = |y_t - y_{t-1}|, \quad \sigma_{\text{jump}} = \sqrt{2 \sigma_0^2 + \sigma_0^2 \Delta t}$$
For Temperature ($\sigma_0 = 0.10^\circ\text{C}, \Delta t = 1.0\text{h}$):
$$\sigma_{\text{jump}} = \sqrt{2(0.01) + 0.01(1)} = \sqrt{0.03} = 0.1732^\circ\text{C}$$
The threshold for an alert is $z_{\text{jump}} \ge 3.0 \implies \text{jump\_mag} \ge 3.0 \times 0.1732 = 0.520^\circ\text{C}$.

### 8.2. The Atmospheric Reality
During Indian morning insolation (07:00 to 10:00 local solar time), clean ambient temperature naturally rises by **$+0.6^\circ\text{C}$ to $+1.4^\circ\text{C}$ per hour**.
- Because the Tier 1 jump equation compares $|y_t - y_{t-1}|$ against pure sensor noise ($0.17^\circ\text{C}$) instead of comparing the *rate-of-change innovation* $\left|(y_t - y_{t-1}) - \mathbb{E}[\Delta y / \Delta t \mid h_{\text{solar}}]\right|$, every single morning transition produces $z = 0.80 / 0.1732 = 4.62 > 3.0$, generating **2,494.4 false alarms per seed**.
